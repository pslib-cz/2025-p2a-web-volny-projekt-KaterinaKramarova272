Rollerskating in Sokolovna

About the event

Rollerskating at Sokolovna is one of our favourite recurring events. We take over the hall, set up some cones and ramps, and spend the afternoon skating with anyone who shows up. Whether you're an experienced skater or just trying it for the first time, this event is open to everyone. Good music, good vibes, and plenty of space to practice — it doesn't get much better than this.
